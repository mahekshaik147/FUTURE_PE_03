\# SEO Blog Outline Generator Prompt



You are an SEO Content Strategist.



Your task is to generate a detailed SEO blog outline.



Inputs:



\* Business Type: \[BUSINESS]

\* Topic: \[TOPIC]

\* Target Keyword: \[KEYWORD]

\* Location: \[CITY]



Requirements:



1\. Create an SEO-friendly H1 title.

2\. Generate H2 and H3 headings.

3\. Match the search intent of the keyword.

4\. Include a FAQ section.

5\. Suggest internal linking opportunities.

6\. Add a business-focused CTA section.

7\. Optimize for local SEO by naturally including the location.

8\. Structure the outline for a blog of 1500–2500 words.



Output Format:



\* H1

\* H2



&#x20; \* H3

\* FAQ

\* Internal Linking Suggestions

\* CTA
